Daily Reminders ♡

Just a few little things I want you to remember.

01

Don't forget to eat

Please don't skip your meals, okay? I know sometimes you get busy or you don't feel like eating but please take care of yourself. Your health matters to me more than you know.

♡ a little reminder from me
02

Please get enough rest

You don't always have to keep going. It's okay to stop for a while and rest. Sleep well and give yourself the rest that you deserve.

♡ rest is important too
03

Don't be too hard on yourself. You don't have to have everything figured out. I'm proud of you for continuing even when things get difficult.

♡ this one is important
